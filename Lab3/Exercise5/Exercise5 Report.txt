Similarly to Exercise3, we see a difference depending on the amount of mutants generated.
For 4000 mutants we get:
FollowsFrom 2 [1,3]
FollowsFrom 2 [1,4]
FollowsFrom 2 [3,4]
FollowsFrom 2 [3,5]
FollowsFrom 3 [1,4]
FollowsFrom 3 [1,2,5]
FollowsFrom 4 [1,3]

FollowsFrom 4 [3,5]
FollowsFrom 5 [1,3]
FollowsFrom 5 [1,4]

while for 1000000 mutants we get:
FollowsFrom 2 [1,4]
FollowsFrom 3 [1,4]
FollowsFrom 4 [1,2,3]
FollowsFrom 5 [1,2,3]
FollowsFrom 5 [1,4]
FollowsFrom 5 [2,4]
FollowsFrom 5 [3,4]

This matches our result from Exercise3, that the conjecture for the smallest subset of properties
is [1,4].

After adding the additional property, which is equivalent to property 1, we adjusted the implication condition to have at least 2 antecedents.
Otherwise 1 == 2 would also appear as 1 => 2 and 2 => 1, which is unwanted.
