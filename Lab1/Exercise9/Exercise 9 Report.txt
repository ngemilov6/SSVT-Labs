Design report:
We implemented a function to find counterexamples to the conjecture that the product of the first n primes plus one is always prime.
We used the definitions for prime and primes from lecture 1 to create an infinite list of prime products.

Then we created an infinite list of counterexamples by iterating through the integers [1..], taking the corresponding slice of primes,
calculating the product of that slice, and checking if the next integer (product + 1) is not prime.
If it is not prime, we add the list of primes and the contradicting number to the output.
