Time spent: 100 min

Design Report:
p after sigma is the set of states that can be reached from the initial state by performing the
observable trace sigma, with any number of tau steps before and after every visible label.
after starts with the tau-closure of the initial state and folds a step function over the trace:
- a visible label moves to all successors with that label, followed by their tau-closure;
- delta keeps the quiescent states of the current set (the delta self-loop of the suspension automaton),
  so after also works for straces.
The result is returned as a set (sorted, no duplicates), so results can be compared with ==.
after is declared infix (infix 5 `after`) and used as lts `after` trace, like the notation in the paper.
The helpers tauClosure and isQuiescent come from Exercise 3, so after and straces agree on what
quiescence and tau-closure mean.

Test Report:
Approach: we combine three kinds of tests.
- Unit tests on models from the paper, where the expected state sets can be worked out by hand. They are
  chosen for the hard cases: a tau step (coffeeModel6), non-determinism (tretmanK3) and delta.
- Properties from the definition: after must be non-empty for every trace and every strace, and empty
  for everything else. The PDF asks for both traces and straces. Traces come from the provided traces
  function (an independent source, so this is not our code checking itself), and straces come from
  the random walk generator of Exercise 3.
- Structural properties that every correct after satisfies, whatever the model: compositionality, only
  existing states, and only quiescent states after delta.
Random sequences of labels test the negative side: about a third of them are straces and the rest are
not, so both outcomes of after are covered.

Properties, tested with traces (LTS.hs traces with tau removed) and with straces (Exercise 3):
- prop_KnownAfter: coffeeModel6 `after` ["coin"] == [2,3] (tau step), coffeeModel6 `after` ["coin","delta"]
  == [3], tretmanK3 `after` ["but"] == [1,2] (non-determinism), tretmanK1 `after` ["but","liq"] == [2],
  tretmanK1 `after` ["delta"] == [0], tretmanK1 `after` ["but","delta"] == [], tretmanS4 `after` ["a"] == [1,3].
- prop_AfterEmptyTrace: after [] is the tau-closure of the initial state and contains it.
- prop_AfterTracesNonEmpty: after every trace gives a non-empty set.
- prop_AfterStracesNonEmpty: after every generated strace gives a non-empty set.
- prop_AfterEmptyIffNotStrace: for random label sequences (34% are straces), after is empty iff the
  sequence is not an strace.
- prop_AfterCompositional: after (sigma ++ rho) equals stepping rho from after sigma.
- prop_AfterSubsetOfStates: after only returns states of the IOLTS.
- prop_AfterDeltaQuiescent: after a delta only quiescent states remain.

QuickCheck output: all properties +++ OK, passed 100 tests (prop_KnownAfter: passed 1 test;
prop_AfterEmptyIffNotStrace: 34% is strace).
